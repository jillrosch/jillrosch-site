About Me

ABOUT ME

I'm a web developer and designer with experience across front-end development, back-end functionality, UI/UX, SEO, and analytics.

I build and manage responsive websites, develop custom functionality, troubleshoot technical issues, and work closely with marketing teams to improve both user experience and website performance. I also work with GA4 and other analytics tools to understand user behavior and make data-informed improvements.

My work isn't limited to the front end. I've built custom forms and QA systems involving structured data, scoring logic, dynamic content, and automated report generation. I enjoy working on the technical side of websites just as much as the visual side.

I'm a hands-on developer who likes figuring things out, solving problems, and finding practical solutions—whether that means fixing a stubborn mobile issue, improving SEO, building a custom tool, or optimizing how a website functions.

I care about building websites that are functional, responsive, maintainable, and useful—not just good-looking.
