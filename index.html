# Market analysis

Competitors mainly target high-end beauty shoppers. On top of that, their visual identity tends to feel very generic and corporate: minimal illustration work, quite "cold", especially when clean beauty or sustainability is the focus.

# Problem

The target audience is wide. So we have to speak to every kind of beauty lover without alienating the segment we call "clean beauty enthusiasts".

Highlight the product
Emphasizing its benefits allows us to connect with a diverse audience of beauty lovers.
Light and warm page
Thanks to the colors, borders, and illustrations that create a welcoming experience.
